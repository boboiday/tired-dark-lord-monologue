A 99-word villain monologue for make.fun's "The Tired Dark Lord" bounty. A dark lord, thirty years into his conquests, is interrupted at midnight by a sword-drawn hero — mid-ledger. The tiredness lives in the deflections: a map that keeps folding wrong, a candle drowning in its own wax, cold tea, and a raven that brought the same report twice. He is still a villain with a scheme (the eastern gate falls Tuesday, the wells by Thursday, the kingdom by Sunday) — he just needs the paperwork filed first.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

The monologue is printed to stdout and written to `/output/entry.txt`. It is validated to be 1–600 characters (577) and about 100 words (99).
